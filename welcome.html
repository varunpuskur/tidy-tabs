Next time, use the broom

Pin it first: click the puzzle piece 🧩 in your toolbar, then the pin next to Tidy Tabs. After that, click the broom or press Alt+T to tidy, and Alt+Z to undo. Right-click the broom for Settings.

On Dia: it tidies the space you're looking at. Right-click a tab you want left alone and pick Never tidy this tab.
