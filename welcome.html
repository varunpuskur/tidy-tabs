🧹 Welcome to Tidy Tabs

Two minutes of setup, then one click sorts your tabs into named groups.

1

Pick how to group

Topic grouping needs a small AI model. Nothing goes through any server of mine.

2

Get a free Gemini key

Open aistudio.google.com/apikey → Create API key → copy it (starts with AIza). Free tier covers hundreds of tidies a day.

3

Tidy

Clicking the broom tidies immediately — no popup. Or press Alt+T. Right-click the broom for Settings, Undo, Ungroup and Auto-tidy.

Using Dia? Dia keeps every space in one window and doesn't tell extensions which is which, so by default Tidy Tabs touches only tabs you've used in the last 2 hours. Change this under Scope in Settings. Mark pinned tabs with right-click → Never tidy this tab to keep them safe.
