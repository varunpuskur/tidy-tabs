🧹 What's new in Tidy Tabs

Thanks for using it. Here's what changed. This page only shows up for bigger updates.

  • Fixed "no tabs found" in Chrome. Chrome was being mistaken for Dia, so it only looked at a tab or two. It now tidies the whole window, like it should.
  • Better space detection in Dia. Tabs you open in a space now count as part of that space right away.
  • Groups by topic, not website. Two Google Docs about different projects now land in different groups.
  • Much more accurate grouping. One group per thing you're working on: a trip is one group, not "flights" and "sightseeing". Email, calendar and one-off pages are left alone. New tabs join the group they belong to.
  • Works on huge windows. 100+ tabs used to leave most of them ungrouped. Not any more.
  • Adding a key fixes old website groups. Groups made without AI get re-sorted by topic on your next tidy.
  • Preview before applying (optional, in Settings): see the groups first, then click Apply.
  • New users get 10 free tidies before needing a key, so your friends can try it in one click.
